    Ideas

    Understand the audience and the one thing they need to know, feel or do.

  2. 00:00:08:12

    Concepts

  3. 00:00:17:04

  4. 00:00:26:18

About

Creativity, communication, technology and learning

I’m a designer, video editor and instructional designer who works where creative communication meets learning.

Today I work in Learning & Development at Singer Sri Lanka PLC, where I create digital learning content, product videos, instructional videos, animations and e-learning materials.

Before that I worked in advertising as an Assistant Producer at Ogilvy Sri Lanka, which taught me how campaigns and productions come together, from brief to final delivery.

I studied Mass Communication at the University of Kelaniya and I’m now reading for a Master of Business Management in Marketing there, which shapes how I think about audiences, brands and trust.
