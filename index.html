How I work

From idea to experience

Every project moves through the same five stages, whether the output is a poster, a product film or an e-learning module.

  1. 00:00:00:00

    Ideas

    Understand the audience and the one thing they need to know, feel or do.

  2. 00:00:08:12

    Concepts

    Turn the message into a creative idea, a script or a learning structure.

  3. 00:00:17:04

    Visuals

    Design the look: typography, layout, colour, storyboards and motion.

  4. 00:00:26:18

    Stories

    Edit and sequence so the message lands in the right order and pace.

  5. 00:00:35:00

    Experiences

    Deliver it where people meet it: screen, stage, social feed or LMS.

I don’t simply create visuals. I solve communication problems through visual storytelling, design, video and learning experiences.

About
